WELCOME TO OPTICAL WORKSHOP

Good work. Honest care.
Right here in town.

Gray’s Jewelers is a family-owned repair shop on the square in downtown McDonough, Georgia. Bring in a ring that needs resizing, a watch that has stopped, or glasses that snapped at the hinge. Most repairs are finished within 24 hours, and the work is done on site rather than shipped away.

Our bench handles jewelry repair, watch repair and battery replacement, and eyeglass and frame repair for customers across McDonough, Stockbridge, Hampton, Locust Grove and the rest of Henry County. Walk in at 39 Griffin St. or call (678) 583-0017 for a free repair estimate.
