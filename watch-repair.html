FAQ +
How long does watch repair take?

Most repairs are finished within 24 hours. Servicing and restoration work takes longer — we will give you a timescale when you drop it off.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in during opening hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, and Saturday, 9am to 3pm. We are at 39 Griffin St, on the square in downtown McDonough.

Can you repair luxury watches?

Yes. Our watchmakers handle everyday watches and luxury pieces alike, from battery replacement through to full servicing and restoration.

Will I get an estimate before the repair?

It depends on what the watch needs. Bring it in and we will give you an estimate before any work starts.
