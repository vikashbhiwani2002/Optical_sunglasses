FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shipping, payments, orders, returns, and our repair services.

01

Shipping

Do you offer shipping for jewelry purchases?

Yes, we offer secure shipping for jewelry purchases across the United States. Every order is carefully packaged to ensure your jewelry arrives safely and in perfect condition.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 3–7 business days, depending on your location. Expedited shipping options may also be available at checkout for faster delivery.

Do you provide tracking information?

Yes. Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email so you can monitor your package until it arrives.

Is my jewelry insured during shipping?

Yes, all shipped jewelry orders are fully insured and securely packaged to protect against damage or loss during transit.

Can I pick up my order in store?

Absolutely. Customers can choose in-store pickup at our McDonough location to avoid shipping charges and receive their items faster.

02

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept several secure payment options including:

  • Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover)
  • Debit cards
  • Secure online payments
Is online payment secure?

Yes. All payments are processed through secure encrypted payment gateways to ensure your personal and financial information is protected.

Can I pay in store?

Yes. Customers visiting our store in McDonough can pay using cash, credit cards, or debit cards.

03

Orders & Returns

What is your return policy?

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your order, you may request a return or exchange within a limited return period provided the item is unused and in its original condition.

Do you accept repairs for items purchased elsewhere?

Yes. Our experienced jewelers can repair many types of jewelry, watches, and eyeglasses—even if they were not originally purchased from our store.

Still have questions?

We’re happy to help.

Contact our team or visit us in McDonough for assistance.
